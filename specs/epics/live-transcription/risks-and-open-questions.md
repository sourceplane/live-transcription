# live-transcription — risks and open questions

| ID | Risk | State | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LT-A | `baseline-v1` is not yet proven live | OPEN | Proven offline in Baseline CI: the offline bootstrap with the blueprint's own hooks, the stage lane's own steps in a staged job tree (with the Deepgram key set and kept out of the build), the dry run bundling `worker.ts` and binding Workers AI as AI, the Worker served by `wrangler dev` and smoke-tested with the component's own command. A console bootstrap closes it, including the first deploy with an AI binding and a custom entry, and the first `wrangler secret bulk` from a deploy lane |
| LT-B | No real model has heard speech | OPEN | The socket, the audio and the transcripts are proven against an in-process stand-in that answers `run(model, params, { websocket: true })` with a WebSocket and Deepgram's `Results`, the shape Cloudflare's own Nova-3 client (`agents`' voice transcriber) reads. Unproven: that Workers AI's Nova-3 socket accepts a browser's linear16 at its AudioContext's rate (44.1 or 48 kHz), as that client sends at its own rate, and how well it hears. Speaking to a deployment closes it; `listenParams` in `lib/listen.ts` is where the options are |
| LT-C | A quiet session may close | WATCH | Deepgram's own socket closes after about 10 s without audio unless sent a KeepAlive; Workers AI's is undocumented on this. The page streams silence too (the worklet never stops), and sends no KeepAlive on Workers AI. A deployment left open muted closes it |
| LT-D | Setting a secret redeploys nothing | ACCEPTED | The deploy is what pushes the Deepgram key to the Worker; the owner redeploys by hand or with the `# orun: redeploy` stamp in `apps/web/component.yaml` (README, "Use your own Deepgram key") |
| LT-E | The page has no sign-in | ACCEPTED | As upstream: anyone who reaches it transcribes, billed to the Cloudflare account (Workers AI, per audio minute) or the Deepgram key. Access or a rate limit is the owner's next step |
| LT-F | The template ships the ABC Favorit Bold font files with no licence of their own | ACCEPTED | `app/fonts/ABCFavorit-Bold.*` are in upstream's MIT repository and loaded by its layout; carried as upstream has them. An owner who needs a clear font licence replaces them |
| LT-G | Next and OpenNext move together | WATCH | OpenNext 1.20.16 accepts Next `>=15.5.29 <16`; a later OpenNext may raise the floor |
| LT-H | The socket's origin check stops other sites' pages, not scripts | ACCEPTED | A browser always sends `Origin`; a script can send any, or none. The check keeps other websites from spending the account's Workers AI through a visitor's browser |
| LT-I | stack-tectonic 0.19.0 kept | ACCEPTED | 0.20.0's lane-scoped install would meet Turbopack on a Next 16 move; on 15.5 it is harmless, and the pin matches the family |
| LT-J | The page in a real browser is checked on Linux only | ACCEPTED | Headless Chrome's fake microphone (`--use-file-for-fake-audio-capture`) never resolves `getUserMedia` on macOS; the e2e drives the page on GitHub's Linux runners, and on macOS says it did not. The client and the worklet are unit-tested on any machine |
